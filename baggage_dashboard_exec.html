執行摘要Executive Summary

① 全網現況① Network Status

  • 異常結構由破損主導：破損占 76.9%，與全球「延誤主導、破損僅 18%」相反。
  • 起程異常率 2.19‰ 低於亞太基準 3.41‰，惟延誤案恐未完整入庫，不宜逕判優於業界。
  • 異常率逐月 2.63 → 2.29 → 2.47 → 2.10 → 2.07 → 1.97 → 1.87（-28.9%）。已排除航網組合與結案延遲兩種假象，但本期無對應改善專案，尚不可歸因為營運改善。
  • The mishandling structure is dominated by damage: damaged cases make up 76.9%, the reverse of the global picture, where delay dominates and damage is only 18%.
  • The departure mishandled rate of 2.19 per 1,000 pax sits below the Asia-Pacific benchmark of 3.41, but delayed cases may not be fully captured, so this should not be read as outperforming the industry.
  • Monthly rate 2.63 → 2.29 → 2.47 → 2.10 → 2.07 → 1.97 → 1.87 (-28.9%). The network-mix and closing-lag artefacts have both been ruled out, but there was no corresponding improvement project this period, so it cannot yet be attributed to operational improvement.

② 最需關注② Where to Focus

  • 美國線（率）：SEA 10.17、LAX 9.40、SFO 6.48‰ 居全網前三；五站僅占運量 6.0% 卻占可控案件 21.2%。
  • TPE（量）：起程案件 1,978 件最大、率僅 1.28——屬母基地規模效應。
  • 北美（時效）：PHX 結案 45.8 天（56 件）、SFO 19.0、LAX 16.0，全網僅 5.2 天。
  • U.S. routes (rate): SEA 10.17, LAX 9.40, SFO 6.48 per 1,000 pax — the top three in the network; the five stations carry only 6.0% of traffic yet account for 21.2% of JX-controllable cases.
  • TPE (volume): the largest departure-station case count at 1,978 cases, with a rate of only 1.28 — the scale effect of the home base.
  • North America (Days to Close): PHX 45.8 days (56 cases), SFO 19.0, LAX 16.0, against a network average of only 5.2 days.

③ 本期財務風險③ Financial Risk This Period

  • 跨航理賠已逾期 36 件 · US$875.96，超過索賠期限，恐無法向他航收回。
  • 資料收集中 246 件 · US$6,862.43 帳齡持續增長，須搶在期限前補件催收。
  • 未收回合計 US$7,738.39。案件高度集中於少數他航夥伴，屬可制度化解決的問題。
  • Interline claims expired: 36 cases · US$875.96 — past the claim deadline and at risk of not being recovered from the other carriers.
  • Collecting documentation: 246 cases · US$6,862.43. The ageing keeps growing; documents must be completed and recovery pursued before the deadlines.
  • Total not yet recovered: US$7,738.39. The cases are highly concentrated among a few interline partners, which makes this a problem that can be solved by process.

前三大建議方向Top 3 Suggested Directions

01

以「美國線起程站」為單位重整收運與配載決策Rebuild acceptance and load-control decisions around the U.S. departure stations

美國五站是全網唯一可以用「一個決策改變一整群案件」的著力點。方向：逐班檢視長程返台班次的載重與實際拉下品項，釐清旅客行李相對於貨物的拉下順序，並要求拉下時回填拉下站（現行系統不記錄，事後無法追溯）。

The five U.S. stations are the one place in the network where a single decision changes a whole group of cases. Direction: review flight by flight the load and the items actually offloaded on long-haul services back to Taipei, clarify the offload order of passenger baggage relative to cargo, and require the offloading station to be recorded whenever a bag is pulled (the current system does not record it, so it cannot be traced afterwards).

觀察指標：美國五站起程異常率由 7.72‰ 降至 4.0‰ 以下Metric to watch: departure mishandled rate at the five U.S. stations falls from 7.72 per 1,000 pax to below 4.0
02

回溯 3 月 LAX 單月暴衝事件Trace back the single-month spike at LAX in March

LAX 3 月起程異常率飆升且成因高度集中於設備故障與艙位／重量限制，其中單一航班即占多數。這是本期最明確的單一事件訊號，也是最容易查清楚的類型。方向：調閱該月裝載紀錄與行李系統維修紀錄，確認屬單一設備故障期或常態性作業問題。

The departure mishandled rate at LAX surged in March, with causes highly concentrated in equipment failure and space/weight restrictions, and a single flight accounting for most of them. This is the clearest single-event signal of the period and the easiest type to establish. Direction: pull that month's loading records and the baggage system maintenance log to confirm whether it was a one-off equipment outage or a recurring operational problem.

觀察指標：兩週內得出明確結論；若屬常態性問題須另立改善案Metric to watch: a firm conclusion within two weeks; if the problem proves recurring, a separate improvement case is required
03

縮短北美理賠授權鏈Shorten the North America claim authorization chain

PHX 平均結案 45.8 天、全網僅 5.2 天。結案慢不影響行李能否找回，但直接影響旅客感受與客訴升級風險，且改善成本低——瓶頸在授權門檻與文件往返，不需新增設備或人力。方向：盤點北美各站賠付核決層級，將小額賠付下放站級核決，並對逾時未結案者建立每週追蹤清單。

PHX averages 45.8 days to close, against a network average of only 5.2 days. Slow closure does not affect whether a bag is recovered, but it directly affects how passengers experience the case and the risk of complaint escalation — and it is cheap to improve: the bottleneck is the approval threshold and the exchange of documents, not equipment or headcount. Direction: review the payout approval levels at each North America station, delegate low-value payouts to station level, and set up a weekly follow-up list for cases still open past their target.

觀察指標：北美各站平均結案天數降至 7 天以下；無單一案件超過 30 天Metric to watch: average Days to Close at the North America stations falls below 7 days, with no single case beyond 30 days
口徑聲明：主指標為起程站（行李進入 JX 掌控的第一站），非旅客報案站；月份以建檔月歸戶，案件一經建檔即不再變動。異常率一律剔除他航交接失敗（RL 54／77）。結案天數僅計已結案案件，近期月份會因未結案案件尚未納入而偏低。
